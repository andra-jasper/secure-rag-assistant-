Northwind Cloud — Refund Policy
===============================

Last updated: March 2026

Eligibility
-----------
You may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the service
was not activated. After activation, refunds are prorated based on the
unused portion of the billing period.

How to request a refund
-----------------------
1. Sign in to the Northwind Cloud console.
2. Open Billing, then select Invoices.
3. Click "Request refund" next to the invoice and describe the reason.

Our billing team reviews refund requests within 5 business days. Approved
refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business
days of approval.

Exceptions
----------
Custom enterprise contracts follow the terms in the signed agreement and
are not covered by this policy. Usage-based overages are non-refundable
once the resources have been consumed.
