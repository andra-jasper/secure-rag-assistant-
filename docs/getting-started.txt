Northwind Cloud — Getting Started Guide
=========================================

Welcome! This guide walks you through launching your first project.

Step 1: Create an account
-------------------------
Sign up with your work email. You will receive a verification link that
expires after 24 hours.

Step 2: Install the CLI
-----------------------
Download the Northwind CLI for your platform and run `northwind login`.
The CLI stores a short-lived token in your home directory.

Step 3: Deploy your first service
---------------------------------
Run `northwind deploy --name hello-world`. The deploy command packages
your code, uploads it, and returns a public URL in under two minutes.

Step 4: Invite your team
------------------------
Open Project Settings, then Members, and invite teammates by email.
Each member gets the Viewer role by default; promote to Editor when
they need to change configuration.
