Northwind Cloud — Security FAQ
==============================

How is my data encrypted?
-------------------------
All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256.
Encryption keys are rotated automatically every 90 days.

Do you store my password?
-------------------------
No. Passwords are stored as salted bcrypt hashes. Our employees can
never see your plaintext password.

Will support ever ask for my credentials?
-----------------------------------------
Never. Northwind Cloud support will never ask for your password, API
keys, or one-time codes. If someone claiming to be support asks for
these, treat it as a phishing attempt and report it.

How do I enable two-factor authentication?
------------------------------------------
Open Account Settings, then Security, and turn on two-factor
authentication. We support authenticator apps and hardware keys.
